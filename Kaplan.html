About Kaplan

Kaplan International Pathways is an organisation that helps international students prepare for & gain admission to universities around the world. It offers pathway courses, including degree preparation & English language support, to help students meet academic & language requirements for university study. Kaplan partners with many universities in countries such as the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, & New Zealand, giving students access to a wide range of degree options. The organisation also provides support throughout the application process, including advice on choosing courses, applying to universities, & settling into student life abroad. Its goal is to help students successfully progress to university & build strong academic & career futures.
