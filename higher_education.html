Nottingham Trent University is a large public university in Nottingham, England, known for its strong focus on practical learning & employability. It offers a wide range of courses, especially strong in business, art & design, & media. NTU is also recognized for its modern facilities & strong links with employers.

De Anza College is a public community college located in Cupertino, California, USA. It is well known for its high transfer rate to top universities such as UC Berkeley & UCLA. The college offers two-year associate degrees and certificates in many academic and career fields.

De Anza College

Liverpool John Moores University is a public university in Liverpool, England, with a strong emphasis on career-focused education and research. It is known for subjects like engineering, sports science, business, & maritime studies. LJMU has strong industry connections and provides many opportunities for internships and practical experience.

Kaplan International Pathways is an education provider that offers pathway courses designed to help international students enter universities in countries like the UK, USA, Canada, and Australia. These courses prepare students academically and improve their English so they can meet university entry requirements. After completing a Kaplan pathway programme, students can progress directly to a degree at one of Kaplan’s partner universities, such as Nottingham Trent University or the University of Liverpool.
