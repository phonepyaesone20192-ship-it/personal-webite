ABOUT CONCEPTX INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

  • ConceptX is an online learning platform focused on improving education, especially for Grade 11 students, by providing structured video courses & learning resources.

  • The school's vision is to educate people through open & compassionate education, helping students grow intellectually & prepare for the future.

  • Courses are taught by experienced instructors who graduated from countries such as the United States, Thailand, & Malaysia, ensuring high-quality teaching.

  • ConceptX focuses on helping students understand concepts deeply rather than memorising, using carefully explained lessons from easy to advanced levels.

  • The platform provides educational videos, ebooks, & affordable learning resources to support students & improve access to quality education.
